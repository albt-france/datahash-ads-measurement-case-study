A retailer in the Gulf: +3.2% incremental orders with Web CAPI on Meta

SIMULATED

The challenge

Browser tracking was losing conversions, so Meta optimised on a partial signal. The team wanted to know what a server-side signal would change in the business, not only in the platform dashboard.

What changed

Web CAPI went live in KSA on 12 Apr 2026, deduplicated with the pixel on event_id. UAE stayed on the pixel.

Results

Meta-reported conversions rose +18.4%. The business result is smaller and is the one that matters: +3.2% orders against the control market, about $289k of revenue in 7 weeks.

How we measured it

> Baseline: 52 weeks of orders in both markets. Intervention: KSA only, 10-day ramp-up excluded. Result: +3.2% (95% interval +1.7% to +4.6%) over 7 weeks fixed in advance. Attribution logic: ratio to the control market (shared calendar), in-time placebos, platform figures on 7-day click by click date.
