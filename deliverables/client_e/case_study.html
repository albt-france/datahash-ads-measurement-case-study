A bank and insurer in the Gulf: +5.4% incremental approved applications with CRM Conversions API on Meta

SIMULATED

The challenge

Browser tracking was losing conversions, so Meta optimised on a partial signal. The team wanted to know what a server-side signal would change in the business, not only in the platform dashboard.

What changed

CRM Conversions API went live in UAE on 3 May 2026, deduplicated with the pixel on event_id. KSA stayed on the pixel.

Results

Meta-reported conversions rose +7.4%. The business result is smaller and is the one that matters: +5.4% approved applications against the control market, about $433k of revenue in 8 weeks.

How we measured it

> Baseline: 52 weeks of approved applications in both markets. Intervention: UAE only, 14-day ramp-up excluded. Result: +5.4% (95% interval +3.4% to +7.4%) over 8 weeks fixed in advance. Attribution logic: ratio to the control market (shared calendar), in-time placebos, platform figures on 7-day click by click date.
